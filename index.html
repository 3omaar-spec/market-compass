How signals work. Every 4-hour candle of each asset is scored 0–10 on eight checks: the 20-day trend, medium and short trends, MACD momentum, RSI, trend strength (ADX), volume and Bitcoin's direction. When the score reaches your minimum, the app replays the same rule over the last ~80 days and counts how often the first target was hit before the stop. That count, blended with the rate across all 100 coins, is the hit chance. Stops are 1.5× the average 4-hour range (ATR). Target 1 is the same distance (1:1). Target 2 is double (2:1). A trade that reaches neither within 3 days is closed.
Read this before trading. Hit chance is history, not a promise. A 65% setup still loses about one trade in three, and losing streaks of four or five happen. Past results ignore fees (~0.1% per side) and slippage. Never risk money you need. Leverage multiplies losses as fast as gains. This app gives information, not financial advice.
